Volcanic Heartland — four days

4 days · 3 nightsAccommodation includedMax 11ModerateCrossing day: Oct–May

Four days, one loop

This is the tour we built the company for. Geothermal country, the big lake, a volcano crossing, and the roads between them that coaches don’t take — with the van never more than eleven people full, and the evenings unhurried.

Day 1 · Auckland → Rotorua
Waitomo dark, Rotorua steam.

Glowworm caves mid-morning, into geothermal country by afternoon, mud pools at golden hour.

Night: Rotorua, twin-share.
Day 2 · Rotorua → Taupō
Wai-O-Tapu early, then the lake.

The thermal park before the buses, Huka Falls, and an afternoon that’s yours — spa, bike, or nothing at all.

Night: Taupō, twin-share.
Day 3 · Tongariro
The Crossing, guided, at your group’s pace.

In alpine season (Oct–May) this is the full Alpine Crossing with your guide walking it beside you. Outside the season it becomes the lower-mountain day — Taranaki Falls and Tama Lakes as conditions allow.

Night: National Park village, twin-share.
Day 4 · The long way home
River country, one last swim, Auckland by evening.

And the group’s chosen stop — 45 minutes, anywhere on the route, your call.

  • Guide + van, all four days

  • Photos of all four days

Asked, answered

Can I cancel?

Day tours: free to 24 hours before pickup, no questions, full refund. Multi-day tours: free to 7 days, half to 72 hours. Weather cancellations on our side are always a full refund or a free rebook — your pick.

Can I talk to someone before booking?

Yes — and not a call centre. WhatsApp us and the guide who’d be driving replies, usually same day. Grill us about fitness, kids, weather, food. Then decide.

What’s this about photos?

Your guide shoots the day on a proper camera — landscapes, the group, you at the good spots. The gallery lands in your inbox that evening, included, no upsell, no watermarks.

How big is the group?

Eleven, never more. One van, one guide who knows your name by the first stop. If we’re not full, we don’t merge you into someone else’s bus — a quiet van is a feature.

What does “first season” pricing mean?

We’re new under our own name, so our rates sit 15–20% under the big operators’ published prices while we earn our reviews. When they arrive, prices step up — booked trips keep their rate.

What’s the guest-chosen stop?

Every day holds 45 minutes the group spends however it likes — a lookout, longer on the sand, the famous pie shop. The group picks, the van goes. It’s your day; we just drive it.

Kids? Fitness? Weather?

Children 5–15 ride at roughly half price on day tours (Waiheke is 18+). Every tour lists its walking grade honestly. Bad weather: we call it early, you choose refund or rebook.

Is the Tongariro Crossing always available?

No — and don’t trust anyone who says otherwise. The Crossing runs October to May. Outside that window our Tongariro days switch to lower-mountain walks, and the site won’t sell you the Crossing in July.
