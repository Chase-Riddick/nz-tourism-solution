# What a North Island operator actually sells, and what it costs

All figures NZD, fetched 2026-08-18, each with its source. Nothing on the site is
invented from nothing: every price on this build sits inside a band triangulated from
the operators below.

## Why this is the highest-risk research on the build

The audience for this site is a **working seasonal tour guide in New Zealand.** He will
sanity-check a day rate in about two seconds, and a wrong one discredits everything
else on the page. Rates are therefore triangulated across four independent
price points rather than taken from any single operator.

## The band this operator sits in

Four reference points, ascending:

```
$269–283 pp/day   packaged multi-day, 16–18 pax, shared/3-star   (Wild Kiwi, mid-range benchmark)
$510      /day    premium day tour, ex-Auckland, big inclusions  (Bush and Beach)
$775+  pp/day     "luxury traveller" all-in benchmark
$950   pp/day     private driver-guide, 2 pax                    (NZ$1,900/day ÷ 2)
```

A two-to-four person operator running **maximum 8–11 guests** cannot price at Wild
Kiwi's $283/day — that rate depends on filling 16 seats. Nor is it a $950/day private
charter. It sits deliberately between them, and the site says why: **the small group
*is* the product**, and a smaller group costs more per head. That is an honest,
checkable argument rather than a number pulled from the air.

Multi-day rates land near **$560–590 pp/day** twin-share — roughly 2× the 16-pax
packaged rate, and ~60% of a private charter. Day tours are priced against Bush and
Beach directly, since that is the closest true comparable.

## Operational realities that constrain the product

These came out of the pricing research and shape what the site can honestly offer:

- **Guide rest days are mandatory.** NZ labour law requires rest days for a
  driver-guide after a given number of driving/working hours, and on private tours the
  client bears that cost even on non-touring days. **For a 2–4 person operation this is
  the binding constraint on how many consecutive multi-day departures are runnable** —
  it is why the site shows a departure calendar with gaps rather than implying daily
  availability on every product.
- **Peak season is a 20–50% cost event, not just a demand event.** Accommodation and
  vehicle costs rise. Either summer rates rise or margin is absorbed; the site takes
  the honest route and shows seasonal pricing.
- **Day tours can run 364 days a year; multi-day tours cannot.** Bush and Beach runs a
  day tour daily because it returns to base nightly. Multi-day departures need
  accommodation blocks and consecutive guide days.
- **Per-head price is extremely group-size sensitive.** $4,399 at 10 pax vs $19,999
  solo on the same 7-day private itinerary — a 4.5× spread. Any published "from" price
  must state the group-size assumption or it is misleading.
