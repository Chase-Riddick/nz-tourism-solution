## Why Cheeky Kiwi is the anchor

Sam guides there today. Same home city, same vehicle class, same max-11 group
size, same product genre. A guest comparing Sam's NZ will be comparing against
CK's published rates, so the derivation must start from them. Grill decision 4
(2026-08-19): launch pricing 15–20% under CK list, loudly temporary.

## Structural findings

1. **CK sells no Tongariro product and nothing longer than 2 days on the North
   Island.** Multi-day depth (2–4 days) and the Crossing are Sam's open lane —
   differentiation without leaving the home market.
2. CK's price ladder is built from third-party inclusions (Hobbiton entry,
   Te Puia, spa, zipline) stacked on the same chassis. Sam's equivalent lever
   is the included photo package and the guest-chosen stop — margin-free
   inclusions no coach can copy.
3. Many CK "tours" are one-way transfers in disguise. Sam's NZ sells only
   return days and true multi-days — simpler promise, simpler ops for two
   people.

## Non-CK anchors

- **Waiheke**: no CK product. Guided full-day wine tour ≈ NZD$370 + $61 ferry
  (Viator "The Essence of Waiheke", fetched 2026-08-21,
  <https://www.viator.com/tours/Waiheke-Island/The-Essence-of-Waiheke-Wine-Tour/d32653-23077P4>);
  boutique 4-vineyard tours from ~$250 before ferry (GetYourGuide category,
  <https://www.getyourguide.com/waiheke-island-l2324/wine-tasting-winery-tours-tc104/>).
  All-in market shape ≈ $310–$430. No struck price shown on site for this tour —
  the comparison is a range, not a list rate.
- **Multi-day per-day rate**: bounded by CK's own NI 2-day ($455/day incl
  accommodation) and SI Southern Highlights ($617/day); ours targets ~$470/day
  at 4 days, landing under both.
