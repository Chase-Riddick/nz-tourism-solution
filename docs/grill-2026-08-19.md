grilled decision by decision. This document supersedes earlier positioning

## Who this is for (sharpened)

Sam is based in **Auckland**, works as a guide at **Cheeky Kiwi Travel**
(cheekykiwitravel.com — the appointed reference site, rated 7–8/10 by Chase).
He and a co-founder — 3–4 people total, 2 guides — are weighing going out on
their own after next season. Sam lived in China for several years. The site is
a private mockup behind an Amplify password for him to react to.
